## 🌐 Overview
**Protocol Engineering (PE)** is a technical framework that facilitates structured interaction with LLMs. Rather than relying solely on raw natural language prompting, PE utilizes predefined structural protocols to coordinate and optimize generative workflows.

Our focus is to address the gap between human associative thinking and LLM sequential text processing, establishing a collaborative framework for high-fidelity human-AI interaction.

## 🧪 Core Concept: "Inference is Compute Consumed"
Protocol Engineering addresses structural characteristics of current LLM architectures:
- **Inference & Search:** Optimization of compute allocated for identifying targets and aligning semantic variables.
- **Generation:** Formatting and post-hoc structured alignment.
- **Protocol Layer:** Acts as a structural bridge to maintain semantic consistency and reduce statistical drift in generative outputs.
